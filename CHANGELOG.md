# Changelog

Every release of m365-aegis and the tasks it closed, newest first. The task lists are generated by
`scripts/changelog.py` from the task files under `tasks/done/` and the release tags: edit the task file,
not the list. The summary under a heading is written by hand and kept by the generator. Each release's zip
(with its third-party licenses) and its `SHA256SUMS.txt` are on the [releases page](https://github.com/speti4/m365-aegis/releases).
